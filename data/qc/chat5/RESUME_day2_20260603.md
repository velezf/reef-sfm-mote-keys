## The headless recipe (PROVEN — use this for ADR-0020 / the real build)

```python
import Metashape as ms
# 1) declare the chunk LOCAL — this is THE lever (kills the WGS84 degree-plane backfill → OOM)
chunk.crs = ms.CoordinateSystem(
    'LOCAL_CS["Local Coordinates (m)",LOCAL_DATUM["Local Datum",0],UNIT["metre",1]]')
# 2) top-down Planar projection. matrix = identity is CORRECT (verified flattest; see tilt note).
proj = ms.OrthoProjection()
proj.crs    = chunk.crs                       # local, so no WGS84 backfill
proj.type   = ms.OrthoProjection.Type.Planar
proj.matrix = ms.Matrix.Diag([1,1,1,1])       # identity (localframe of a LOCAL_CS is identity anyway)
# 3) ESM Step 14 — DEM, 1 cm (ADR-0017), interpolation ENABLED, from the 17.5M-pt cloud
chunk.buildDem(source_data=ms.PointCloudData, interpolation=ms.EnabledInterpolation,
               projection=proj, resolution=0.01)
# 4) ESM Step 15 — Orthomosaic on the DEM, same proj (co-registers exactly)
chunk.buildOrthomosaic(surface_data=ms.ElevationData, blending_mode=ms.MosaicBlending,
                       fill_holes=True, projection=proj)   # resolution=0 → image GSD
```
Export GeoTIFF: `format=ms.RasterFormatTiles, image_format=ms.ImageFormatTIFF`
(source `ms.ElevationData` or `ms.OrthomosaicData`). All enums/signatures confirmed in 2.3.1.

### Why this works (and why the day-2 hypothesis was wrong)
- The orphan forensic (`orphan_1857_note.md`) proves the OOM'd build **already set**
  `proj.type=Planar` **and** `proj.matrix=chunk.transform.matrix`, leaving **`proj.crs` unset** →
  backfilled WGS84 (degrees) → ~3.88e9×1.99e9-cell geographic plane → `std::bad_alloc`.
- So the day-2 premise ("the attempt set `proj.crs` only, never `type`/`matrix`") is **FALSIFIED**.
  `type`/`matrix` are not the lever; the **`chunk.crs`→LOCAL mutation** is (ADR-0018's
  "Alternative considered, not chosen" — the project-wide CRS write).

## The 24° tilt — what is and isn't known

Plane fit of the interp-ON DSM: long-axis **+11.9°** (+2.11 m / 10 m), cross-axis **+21.7°**,
total **24.3°**; detrended rugosity std **0.104 m** (real coral structure survives the detrend).
- Tested 5 `proj.matrix` candidates (identity, region.rot, its transpose, transform.rotation,
  its transpose). **identity is the flattest (24.3°) and the only one giving the correct 10×1 m
  AOI** + relief matching the dense-cloud Z-span. The others are 29–38° and distort the footprint.
  → "wrong projection plane" is **FALSIFIED**; the recipe is correct.
- Therefore the tilt is **inherent to the day-1 Step-11 frame/region placement**, and is
  **method-independent** — a GUI DEM of the same region reproduces it. It does **not** gate the
  headless-vs-GUI decision.
- **Genuine reef slope vs. day-1 leveling error is UNRESOLVED:** the published P13HMEON EDR
  reference DEM is **not on disk** (`data/raw/P13HMEON/` empty; Chat-4 pulled only catalog JSON).
